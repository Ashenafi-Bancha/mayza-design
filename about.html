About

Cultural. Modern. Beautiful.

Mayza Design is a fashion and art design brand based in Addis Ababa.

Our story

Rooted in culture, made for now

Our work starts from Ethiopian cultural dress, and especially from the red, black and yellow woven bands that Sidama clothing is known for.

We carry those colours and patterns into the way people dress today: a wedding dress, a stage outfit, a child's dress or an everyday tee.

Each piece is cut and sewn to order. We aim to offer designs and materials you will not find anywhere else.

What we stand for

Three promises

Respect for the culture

The patterns and colours carry meaning. We use them with care and get them right.

Made to fit

Clothing looks its best when it fits, which is why we sew to your measurements.

Original design

Every collection is our own drawing, starting from tradition and moving somewhere new.
